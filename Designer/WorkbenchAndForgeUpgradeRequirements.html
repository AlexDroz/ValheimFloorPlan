Pro Layout & Base Optimization Strategy

Competitive players hide bulky upgrades to maximize walkable floor area and optimize item pathing inside compact craft rooms.

👇 Under-Floor Burying (Sub-Flooring)

  • Concept: Dig a 1-meter deep crawlspace under your craft room floorboards.
  • Execution: Drop structures like the Chopping Block, Anvils, and Forge Cooler directly into the pit, then snap wood floors cleanly over them.
  • Benefit: Retains 100% upgrade line tethers while keeping floors clear for quick storage-box movement.

🧱 False Walls (Vertical Stacking)

  • Concept: Build a hollow, 1x1 meter double wall behind your active workstations.
  • Execution: Place bulky or wide upgrades (like the Tanning Rack, Bellows, or Tool Shelf) inside this cavity, then enclose them using thin wood paneling or stone curtains.
  • Benefit: Keeps your production stations looking clean while bypassing messy structural collision errors.

🌐 Exterior Venting (Outside Placements)

  • Concept: Exploit tether limits by punching structural upgrades right through outside perimeter walls.
  • Execution: Place large-footprint objects like the Grinding Wheel or Adze immediately outside the house against the outer wall directly behind the indoor workbench.
  • Benefit: Clears interior floor space entirely; just ensure you throw a small roof over them to prevent weather decay.

Workbench 1 Base Station 10 Wood 2x1 ~8m build cylinder. Must be sheltered (roof + enclosing walls) or incurs weather decay penalty. Upgrade nodes tether within ~5m. No boss gate. Available from the very start of a run.
Workbench 2 Chopping Block 10 Wood, 10 Flint 1x1 ~5m cylinder tether. Must place close to the table. Easily buried under floors. Early Meadows. No boss required.
Workbench 3 Tanning Rack 10 Wood, 15 Flint, 20 Leather Scraps, 5 Deer Hide 1x2 ~5m cylinder tether. Tall footprint. Best tucked behind false walls. Meadows hunting tier. No boss gate.
Workbench 4 Adze 10 Fine wood, 3 Bronze 2x1 ~5m cylinder tether. Floor structure. Can be pushed outside through back walls. Kill Eikthyr for Hard Antler → craft Antler Pickaxe → Mine Copper/Tin → Smelt into Bronze.
Workbench 5 Tool Shelf 10 Fine wood, 4 Iron, 4 Obsidian 2x1 ~5m cylinder tether. Wall-mounted unit hanging directly above the station. Normally follows The Elder progression for reliable iron, but can be soft-skipped through alternate iron routes.
Stonecutter - Base Station 2 Iron, 4 Stone, 10 Wood 2x1 ~8m build radius. Standalone station; no tether to other crafting stations. Place on flat ground with clear access for stone-work builds. Requires Iron. Typically gated behind The Elder via Swamp Crypt iron sourcing. Also unlocks the Sharpening Stone needed for the Forge Grinding Wheel.
Forge 1 Base Station 4 Stone, 4 Coal, 10 Wood, 6 Copper 2x2 ~8m build cylinder. Must be sheltered. Built-in forge fire; no separate heat source required. Upgrade nodes tether within ~5m. Requires Copper. Gated behind Eikthyr for the Hard Antler Pickaxe to mine ore.
Forge 2 Forge Anvils 5 Wood, 2 Bronze 1x1 ~5m cylinder tether. Small floor asset. Fits under standard floor tiles. Requires Bronze. Locked behind Eikthyr pickaxe tier.
Forge 3 Forge Cooler 25 Fine wood, 10 Copper 1x1 ~5m cylinder tether. Tall barrel profile. Perfect choice for vertical false walls. Requires Copper mining (Eikthyr) and Fine Wood chopping (requires Bronze Axe).
Forge 4 Smith's Anvil 5 Wood, 20 Iron 1x1 ~5m cylinder tether. Medium floor block. Can sit flat inside sub-floor pits. Requires smelted iron. Usually obtained after The Elder, but not a strict hard gate.
Forge 5 Forge Toolrack 10 Wood, 15 Iron 2x1 ~5m cylinder tether. Wall hanging item. Snaps onto rafters directly over the Forge. Iron tier upgrade. Usually follows The Elder progression but can be soft-skipped.
Forge 6 Grinding Wheel 25 Stone, 1 Sharpening Stone 2x2 ~5m cylinder tether. Enormous rotating unit. Place outdoors against the back partition. Needs Stonecutter + Sharpening Stone chain; typically post-The Elder, but not a strict hard gate.
Forge 7 Forge Bellows 5 Wood, 5 Deer Hide, 4 Chain 2x1 ~4.5m strict cylinder tether. Tight radius limits; hide in closest side wall panels. Requires Chains. Usually crypt-driven progression, but chain can also come from night Wraith drops (soft gate).
Cauldron 1 Base Station 10 Tin 1x1 ~8m build cylinder. Must be suspended above an active Hearth or Bonfire; no fire beneath it means it cannot be used. Upgrade nodes tether within ~5m. Requires Tin. Gated behind Eikthyr pickaxe to mine Black Forest riverbed deposits.
Cauldron 2 Spice Rack 3 Dandelion, 2 Carrot seeds, 5 Mushroom, 3 Thistle, 3 Turnip seeds 2x1 ~5m cylinder tether. Roof-suspended hanging asset above hearth/cauldron. Swamp vegetation access. Skilled runners skip bosses to scavenge Swamp Turnips.
Cauldron 3 Butcher's Table 2 Ancient Bark, 4 Core wood, 4 Fine wood, 2 Silver 2x1 ~5m cylinder tether. Flat workbench counter next to hearth. Needs ground floor room. Commonly unlocked via Bonemass Wishbone path, but silver can be soft-skipped by advanced prospecting methods.
Cauldron 4 Pots and Pans 10 Fine wood, 5 Copper, 5 Iron, 5 Black Metal 3x1 ~5m cylinder tether. Massive wall hanging collection layout item. Place high on rafters. Requires Black Metal. Smelting Black Metal requires Blast Furnace via Artisan chain, making this a practical hard gate at Moder.
Cauldron 5 Mortar and Pestle 8 Black Marble, 6 Fine wood, 4 Core wood 1x1 ~5m cylinder tether. Small table-top asset. Easily blends into standard counter layouts. Typically reached through Yagluth Mistlands flow, though Black Marble acquisition is not strictly hard-gated.
Cauldron 6 Rolling Pins and Cutting Boards 8 Ashwood, 6 Fine wood, 4 Flametal 2x1 ~5m cylinder tether. Compact prep rack that can sit above counters or against side walls. Ashlands tier. Requires reaching Ashlands (typically via The Queen progression for Drakkar access).
Artisan Table 1 Base Station Anchor 10 Wood, 2 Dragon Tear 2x2 40m Build Zone Cylinder Anchor. Centralizes construction across your crafting compound. Kill Moder to obtain Dragon Tears. Central prerequisite layout structure.
Artisan Table 2 Artisan Press 5 Black marble, 5 Bronze, 1 Majestic Carapace 1x1 40m Build Zone Cylinder Anchor. Wall/floor attachment used as the Artisan Table level upgrade node. Ashlands progression. Majestic Carapace is obtained after defeating The Queen.
Industrial Unit - Charcoal Kiln 20 Stone, 5 Surtling Core 2x2 No station tether. Exterior smelting-yard placement is ideal to keep smoke, traffic, and storage flow clean. Black Forest utility structure. Requires Surtling Cores from Burial Chambers or Surtling sources. No hard boss gate.
Industrial Unit - Furnace (Smelter) 20 Stone, 5 Surtling Core 2x2 No station tether. Keep next to Charcoal Kiln and ore chests for compact ore-to-bar throughput. Early smelting chain for Copper/Tin/Iron. Core requirement is Black Forest exploration; no strict boss kill required.
Industrial Unit - Blast Furnace 20 Stone, 5 Surtling Core, 10 Iron, 20 Fine wood 3x3 Artisan Proximity Range (40m). Place outside in an industrial yard setup. Highly bulky. Proximity dependency to Artisan Table (Moder). Used to melt Black Metal and Flametal.
Industrial Unit - Spinning Wheel 20 Fine wood, 10 Iron Nails, 5 Leather Scraps 2x2 Artisan Proximity Range (40m). Requires interior indoor structural sheltering or it decays. Requires proximity to Artisan Table (Moder). Converts Flax harvested into Linen Thread.
Industrial Unit - Windmill 20 Stone, 30 Wood, 30 Iron Nails 3x3 Artisan Proximity Range (40m). Massive exterior tower layout. Do not enclose in standard walls. Proximity dependency to Artisan Table (Moder). Processes Barley into Flour.
Industrial Unit - Stone Oven 20 Stone, 4 Surtling Core, 15 Iron 2x2 Artisan Proximity Range (40m). Requires indoor/covered ventilation kitchen footprint to trap smoke. Proximity dependency to Artisan Table (Moder). Bakes Bread/Lox Pies.
Black Forge 1 Base Station 10 Black marble, 5 Yggdrasil wood, 5 Refined Eitr, 5 Blackheart 2x2 ~8m build cylinder. Must be sheltered. Requires an active Eitr refinery chain nearby for consumable Refined Eitr. Upgrade nodes tether within ~5m. Full Mistlands refinery chain required. Practically gated behind Yagluth for safe Mistlands access.
Black Forge 2 Black Forge Cooler 5 Iron, 5 Copper, 4 Black marble 2x1 ~5m cylinder tether. Intricate wall attachment unit. Best hung above or in adjacent partitions. Mistlands tier. Primarily gated by Mistlands access and refinery chain setup.
Black Forge 3 Vice 5 Iron, 8 Copper, 2 Mechanical Spring 1x1 ~5m cylinder tether. Compact bench-side upgrade attachment. Requires Mechanical Springs from Mistlands (Infested Mine progression loop).
Black Forge 4 Metal Cutter 5 Black marble, 5 Flametal, 5 Ashwood, 4 Charred Bone 1x1 ~5m cylinder tether. Small side fixture; easy to mount along edge lanes. Ashlands tier upgrade branch. Needs post-Drakkar Ashlands material flow.
Black Forge 5 Gem Cutter 5 Flametal, 8 Ashwood, 2 Morgen Sinew, 1 Bloodstone 1x1 ~5m cylinder tether. Small standing table layout item. Place directly beside the main Forge. Kill The Queen to acquire Majestic Carapace → fabricate Drakkar ship hull to cross to Ashlands.
Galdr Table 1 Base Station 10 Fine wood, 5 Black marble, 5 Refined Eitr, 3 Rune Tablet 2x2 ~8m build cylinder. Must be sheltered. Primary Mistlands magic-crafting station. Upgrade nodes tether within ~5m. Rune Tablets drop from Dvergr structures. Requires full Mistlands access; practically gated behind Yagluth.
Galdr Table 2 Rune Table 5 Yggdrasil wood, 10 Black marble, 10 Refined Eitr 1x1 ~5m cylinder tether. Side lectern addition. Needs flat floor space. Requires full Mistlands refinery loop for Refined Eitr (typically reached after Yagluth progression).
Galdr Table 3 Unfading Candles 15 Resin, 10 Black marble, 10 Refined Eitr, 3 Skeleton Trophy 1x1 ~5m cylinder tether. Tall magical candle cluster flanking the main table. Flank both sides. Mistlands magical upgrade loop. Requires basic resource extraction.
Galdr Table 4 Feathery Wreath 3 Ashwood, 10 Refined Eitr, 8 Celestial Feather, 1 Asksvin Trophy 2x1 ~5m cylinder tether. Hanging overhead wreath element. Keeps floor space entirely open. Kill The Queen to penetrate Ashlands → gather Celestial Feathers from Fallen Valkyries.
